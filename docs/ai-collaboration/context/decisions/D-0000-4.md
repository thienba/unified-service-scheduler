The UI follows Keyloop's visual language, taken from https://keyloop.com/: Plus Jakarta Sans for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body text, the Tailwind type scale, navy and carbon with a signal-blue accent, and 12px/8px/pill radii. The tokens are in design/brand/. The Keyloop logo and wordmark are not used. Reason: the human asked to follow the site's font sizes, font families, theme and colours; this is an assessment entry, not an official Keyloop product.
