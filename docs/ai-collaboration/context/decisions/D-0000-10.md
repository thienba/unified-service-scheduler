**Everything written in the repos is in English, in plain, everyday words, the way you would say it in a conversation.** This covers code, comments, commit messages, PR descriptions, the README, the design document, logs (app logs and the AI collaboration log), context files and reports. Short sentences, common words, no jargon where a plain word works. The only exception is `references/`, which keeps the human's messages word for word (with an English translation next to them), because that is the record of what was said. Reason: the human's words, (the human's original message is kept in the private work repo).

**Update 2026-10-28:** the AI collaboration log keeps only entries that earn marks: where the human directed the AI, checked its output, or corrected it. Housekeeping steps (language rules, formatting, presentation prep, cost) are left out. Reason: the human's words ("avoid logging things that don't earn points"), (the human's original message is kept in the private work repo).
