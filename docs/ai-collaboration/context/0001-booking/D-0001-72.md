**Question:** The approved schedule writes short visible words on a block ("Full service" over "CD34 FGH, bay 1"), on a free gap ("1h 30m free") and on a free-gap row ("16:30 to 18:00 · 1h 30m free"), while the accessible names are the long ones in staff.md 5.1 ("09:15 to 12:15, Full service, Vauxhall Corsa CD34 FGH, Maya Okafor, bay 1, Drop-off"; "Free, 16:30 to 18:00, 1 hour 30 minutes, Sofia Marques. Book this time."). WCAG 2.5.3 (Label in Name, level A, part of NFR-4) wants the visible words to appear, in order, inside the name, and axe-core's `label-content-name-mismatch` fails every block and gap as drawn. Which gives way: the visible words or the names?

- A. Keep the names exactly as staff.md writes them and change the visible words to a run of the name: a block shows the service over make, model and plate ("Full service" over "Vauxhall Corsa CD34 FGH"; the bay moves to the detail panel and the list), the one-day view shows the time over the service, a timeline gap shows "Free" (the plus alone below 112 px), and a free-gap row reads "Free, 16:30 to 18:00, 1 hour 30 minutes," over "Sofia Marques. Book this time."
- B. Keep the design's visible words and start each name with them ("Full service, CD34 FGH, bay 1, 09:15 to 12:15, Vauxhall Corsa, Maya Okafor, Drop-off"; "1h 30m free, 16:30 to 18:00, Sofia Marques. Book this time."). The names in staff.md 5.1 and the ticket change.
- C. Keep both as designed and accept the axe finding as a known exception (NFR-4 then has one written exception).

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. The names the ticket and screen-reader users rely on stay word for word, axe reports nothing, and voice-control users can say what they see. The C3 build does A; the visible changes are small and listed in the ticket's Result.
