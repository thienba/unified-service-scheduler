**Question:** On the staff `book` page opened from a tapped free gap, what happens while the gap's technician is still being looked up, or when that look-up fails (500 or no network)? The page reads the technician's name and free window from the day's schedule. Before this fix it went on as if no gap was tapped: the times were for any technician, the summary said "Picked for you", and Confirm sent no technician, without a word. Q-0001-234 item 5 covers only an id the API does not know. The design draws no state for this.

- A. Wait for the technician. While the look-up runs, a loading block ("Loading the gap you tapped", said to a screen reader) shows and the days and times do not load. If it fails, an error block shows in their place: "We could not check the gap you tapped." "Something went wrong on our side, so we cannot show the technician or their times. Nothing was changed. Try again; if it keeps happening, quote reference 4bf92f35." (or "We could not reach the booking service, …" with no network), with Try again. Confirm stays disabled and the summary's Technician row stays empty. "Any technician" in the summary is not shown, so staff leave a failed gap with "Back to customer" or by trying again. The `book-customer` page keeps its quiet behaviour: no gap note while the look-up runs or fails.
- B. As A, and the error block also has "Any technician", which drops the technician from the address and shows the times for anyone.
- C. Go on with any technician, as before, but say so in a warning Alert: "We could not check the gap you tapped, so we show times with any technician."

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A, built now. It never books without the technician staff tapped, and it is the smallest change. B is one button more if staff want a way out without trying again.
