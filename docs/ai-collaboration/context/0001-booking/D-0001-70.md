**Question:** The car choice is to read "Courtesy car, Car 2" on the appointment page and the schedule's detail (REQ-BOOK-010.AC-2, staff.md 5.2). The API's `display` block had no courtesy car name, so the C3 build adds an optional `display.courtesyCarName` (the car's name, sent when the appointment has a courtesy car). The seed names the cars "Courtesy car 1", so the words come out as "Courtesy car, Courtesy car 1". What should the cars be called?

- A. Rename the seeded cars to "Car 1" (London) and "Car 1" (Saigon); the screens keep "Courtesy car, {name}".
- B. Keep the seed names and show the name alone ("Courtesy car 1") where the design writes "Courtesy car, Car 2".
- C. Keep both as they are ("Courtesy car, Courtesy car 1").

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. The design's words stay, and the seed is sample data. The C3 build shows "Courtesy car, {name}" (option A's words) and does not change the seed, which belongs to the store ticket.
