**Question:** Which days may each date control offer? The calendar must show days outside the limits as disabled (crossed out). The booking limits are a minimum lead time (sample: 2 hours) and a furthest booking date (sample: 30 days). Do they apply to every date field, or only to the ones that make a booking?

- A. Booking limits only where a booking is moved.
- B. Lead time and the 30-day horizon on every date field. Simple, but a manager cannot enter leave or holiday hours more than 30 days away.
- C. No limits on any field except "not in the past"; the server refuses a bad day after the user presses Save. Least to design, but the user finds out late.

**Decided:** A, the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. Each field then offers exactly the days that make sense for its job, and a crossed-out day always comes with a sentence that says why.
