**Question:** "In the order the messages were made", "Moved from Fri 30 Oct, 09:15" and "For the old time, …" need, for each message, when it was made and which start it was for. `GET /outbox` sends neither (only `kind`, `dueAt`, `status`), and its order is by random id. The screen works them out: a confirmation, a move and a cancellation are due when they are made; waiting reminders belong to the newest booking or move; cancelled reminders are paired by day and given to the earlier ones; a reminder 48 hours before gives its start exactly, a morning reminder at 08:00 gives only the day. This is right for one booking, one move and one cancel; after two or more moves the old times may be paired with the wrong move, and a morning-only old time shows its day without a time. Should the contract carry the facts instead?

- A. Keep working them out in the screen, as built.
- B. Add `createdAt` and `forStart` to `OutboxMessage` (store, domain, wire, contract; a small backend ticket); the screen then reads them and drops the guessing.

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: B. Two fields, no personal data, and the screen becomes exact for any number of moves. Until then A stays.

Reason: the human's answer ("còn lại theo đề xuất", take the recommendation for the rest), (the human's original message is kept in the private work repo).
