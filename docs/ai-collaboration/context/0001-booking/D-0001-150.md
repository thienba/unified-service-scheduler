**Decided:** when the times arrive, the area keeps its held height while a press is in progress and shrinks to fit only after the press ends. A press is then not lost on any day, including a day with many bookings, without having to count the times exactly. The page can still move once, after the press.

Reason: the human's answer ("Do not shrink during a press"), (the human's original message is kept in the private work repo). The lead measured the option first taken: on a weekday, when not scrolled to the bottom, the car choice jumped 104 px and the press was lost in all three browsers, so that option's claim that a press stays safe was wrong.
