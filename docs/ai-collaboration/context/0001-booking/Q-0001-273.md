**Question:** The held space counts the starts an empty day can have from the dealership's hours, the service
length and the 15-minute grid. That count is exact on a Saturday in the demo (09:00 to 16:00: 25 starts, 25 shown).
On a weekday it is 2 too many: the demo's mechanics' shifts end at 16:30 to 17:30, so a 60-minute job on a
08:00 to 18:00 day has 35 real starts, and the hours say 37. The two extra starts add a group heading and a row,
so on an empty weekday the car choice still moves about 54 px (more than the 24 px in the ticket). The page
cannot see the shifts. What should hold the space on weekdays?

- A. Keep hours only. Accept about 54 px of movement on a weekday. A press is still safe, because the radio stays
  under a finger that lands in its middle, but the ticket's 24 px does not hold on weekdays.
- B. Make the demo's hours match the shifts (close at 17:30 on weekdays). Hours then say 35, which is exact.
  It changes seed data and what the strip says about each day.
- C. Send the earliest start and the latest end of the day's shifts with the dealership (a contract change), and
  count from those.

**Recommended:** B. It keeps the page simple and the count true, and a real dealership's hours are usually the
hours it takes work in. C is the right fix if shifts and hours are meant to differ in real use.

**Taken for now:** A (hours only). Tests use Saturdays, where the count is exact. Nothing else is done for this.

**Answered:** a fourth option, do not shrink during a press (D-0001-150).
