**Question:** What does a new customer need, and what if they already exist?

- A. Required: full name, phone, and the vehicle's registration plate, make and model; optional: email, year. If the phone or the registration matches an existing record, staff are shown it and asked to use it instead of creating a duplicate. The new customer is stored only when the booking is confirmed.
- B. Name and phone only; the vehicle is added later.
- C. No duplicate check.

**Decided:** option A, confirmed (D-0001-112, Q-0001-256 A). It was taken as provisional on the first day, has been built and tested that way since, and later decisions rest on it.
