**Question:** "Past and cancelled" on My appointments should list the customer's past appointments (the newest 10, then "Show earlier"). The API cannot send them: `GET /appointments` without a date gives a customer only the appointments that have not ended yet (`listMyAppointments`, "from now on"), and `includeCancelled=true` adds only the cancelled ones that have not ended yet. With a `date` it gives one day only. So the section can show cancelled appointments that are still ahead, and never a past one. How should past appointments reach the screen?

- A. Add `includePast=true` to `GET /appointments` for customers: their own appointments that have ended, newest first (contract, mock and `listMyAppointments`; a small backend ticket). The screen then shows them with the Past badge, 10 at a time.
- B. Keep the API as it is. "Past and cancelled" shows the cancelled appointments that are still ahead, and the words stay the same. A past appointment is not shown anywhere.
- C. Rename the section to "Cancelled" until A is done.

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A, because the design and REQ-BOOK-003.AC-2 expect a customer to see what they had done, and it is one optional query value. The screen already sorts the section newest first, cuts it at 10 with "Show earlier", and gives an appointment whose end has passed the Past badge, so A needs no screen change. Until you answer, the screen shows what the API sends today (B).
