Each branch's configuration holds its **IANA time zone** (for example `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`, `Europe/London`), chosen by the manager from the branch's location. Opening hours, shifts, appointment times and reminder times are branch-local; an appointment is stored as a UTC instant plus its branch's zone; the customer's browser zone and fixed offsets such as "+7" are never used.
