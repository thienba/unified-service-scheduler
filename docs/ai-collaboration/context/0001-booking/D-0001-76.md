**Question:** Small choices in the dealership settings and shifts screens where the design and the ticket leave a gap. Each is easy to change.

- 1. "Technician of another dealership" (`not-found`) is decided from the dealership's technician list: an id in the address that is not in the list shows "We cannot find that technician." The API's 404 is seen only when a save answers 404 (the contract has no "get one technician").
- 2. The warning counts only bookings the change flags newly: the screen reads the flagged list before the preview and leaves out bookings that were flagged already.
- 3. "Add to the list" on Shifts takes a first day and an optional last day and adds every day between (at most 31 at once), each as its own row.
- 4. The validation summary counts the rows of the add form too, when a date is typed in it but not yet added ("There is already a change for …").
- 5. "Discard changes" (quiet button) sits next to Save from 1024 wide; on a phone the bar has Save only, as drawn.
- 6. With no booking touched, the result reads "Settings saved" / "Shifts saved" with the toasts "Dealership settings saved." and "Shifts saved for <name>."
- 7. The `no-permission` and `no-permission-dealership` states are the shell's (C1); "Show <their dealership>" goes to the manager's schedule, as in the other manager screens.
- 8. The rows of the warning name the reason from the change ("ends 1 hour 30 minutes after closing", "<name> is on leave"); a flag the screen cannot explain shows the API's reason.
- 9. The shifts warning says "One booking is with Demo Tech Ash that day." where the design says "One booking is with her that day." The data has no pronoun for a technician, so the screen repeats the name instead of guessing "her", "him" or "them".

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: keep all nine.
