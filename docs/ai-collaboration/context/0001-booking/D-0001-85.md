**Question:** The search-time histogram is kept per dealership (`availability_search_duration_ms{dealershipId, kind}`), but the panel shows one line per kind ("Day search", "Check one time", "Closest times"). When one tab searched two dealerships, how are the middle and the slowest 5% shown?

- A. Add the counts; show the highest middle and the highest slowest-5% of the dealerships. It never shows a search as faster than it was, and "Within target" is judged on that slowest value (what is built).
- B. One line per dealership and kind (longer list; the design draws one line per kind).
- C. Merge the raw values in the mock so one exact percentile comes back (a contract change).

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. In the demo a tab nearly always works in one dealership, where A is exact; with two it errs on the slow side.
