**Question:** The reschedule screen asks `GET /dealerships/{id}/availability` for each day of the strip. That search counts the appointment being moved as busy, because the contract has no way to leave it out (the reschedule command itself does leave it out: `excludeAppointmentId` in `rescheduleAppointment.ts`). So the times that overlap the customer's own appointment, and need its technician, bay or courtesy car, are missing from the grid, although the move would succeed. customer.md ("For the architect") asked for the search to ignore the appointment being moved. With the seed's three technicians this is rarely seen; on a busy day a customer could not move a job by 15 minutes. What should the search do?

- A. Add an optional `appointmentId` query value to `GET /dealerships/{id}/availability` and to `POST /availability-checks`: that appointment is left out of the check. Only someone who may change it (the owner, or the manager of its dealership) may send it; anyone else gets the same 404 as for a missing id (SEC-5). A small backend ticket; the screen then sends it.
- B. Keep the search as it is. The screen adds the appointment's own start as the "Your time" chip itself, and the overlapping times that only the appointment itself blocks are not offered.

**Decided:** A. `GET /dealerships/{id}/availability` and `POST /availability-checks` take an optional `appointmentId`: that appointment is left out of the check, so the person moving it sees every time it could move to. Only the owner, or staff or a manager of its dealership, may send it; anyone else gets the same answer as for an appointment they cannot see. Everyone else still sees the old time as busy until the move is confirmed. The reschedule screen sends it and drops its own "Your time" workaround where it is no longer needed.
