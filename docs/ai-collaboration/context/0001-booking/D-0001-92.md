**Question:** The new-customer form's duplicate hint (D-0001-30 B) asks `POST /customers/search` with `phone` or `registration` when staff leave the field. The store compares them exactly as they were typed when the customer was saved (`findDuplicates` in the memory and Dexie stores): "+44 7700 900101" finds Demo Customer A, but "07700 900101" or "+447700900101" does not, and "ab00 dma" does not find "AB00 DMA". So the hint misses the same person written another way. Should the exact match ignore spaces, brackets and letter case?

- A. Compare digits only for phones and letters and numbers in capitals for plates, in both stores (a small backend change in its own ticket; the screen does not change).
- B. Keep the exact match as built.

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. The hint exists to catch the same person typed again, and people type numbers and plates in many ways. "+44" against a leading "0" would still differ; matching the last 10 digits is a further step you may want. Until you answer, B stays and the screen sends the trimmed value.
