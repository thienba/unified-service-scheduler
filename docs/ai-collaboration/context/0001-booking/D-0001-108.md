**Question:** My appointments can list appointments at dealerships in different time zones (a customer may book at London and at Saigon). design.md section 9 says the note is shown "once per screen", but one sentence such as "Times are in London time." would be wrong for the Saigon cards. What should the page say when its appointments are in more than one zone?

- A. When every appointment on the page is in one zone, one note under the title ("Times are in London time.", only if the browser is in another zone). When they are in more than one zone, no page note: each card whose zone differs from the browser's adds "(London time)" after its time ("10:00 to 11:00 (London time)").
- B. Always one note per card, never a page note.
- C. One page note that names every zone, for example "Times are in each dealership's own time: London, Ho Chi Minh."

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. The common case (one dealership) keeps the single note the design asks for, and a mixed list never says a wrong zone next to a time. Choose B if you want every card to look the same whatever the list holds; choose C if per-card words feel too busy. The ticket is built as A until you answer (`src/ui/features/my-appointments/MyAppointmentsPage.tsx`, `AppointmentCard.tsx`; test "my appointments at dealerships in two zones" in `tests/e2e/time-zone-note.spec.ts`).
