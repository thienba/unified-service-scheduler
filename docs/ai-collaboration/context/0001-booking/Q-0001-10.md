**Question:** How are technician shifts modelled? (Who keeps them up to date is settled by D-0001-5: the manager.)

- A. A weekly pattern per technician (for example Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 with a 12:00–13:00 break), plus date-specific exceptions (leave, extra shift); the manager edits them (D-0001-5).
- B. A roster of shifts per date only, with no weekly pattern.
- C. A weekly pattern only, with no exceptions or breaks.

**Taken:** A, because a weekly pattern with exceptions is how garages plan shifts and it gives the tests real edge cases (a break, a day off); "staff" here is read as the technicians who do the jobs, since front-desk shifts do not affect availability (provisional, until the brainstorm).
