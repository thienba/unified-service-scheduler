When two people confirm the same bay or technician for overlapping times, **the first confirm wins and the later one is told politely** that the time was just taken, with other times suggested. "First" is the order in which the server (the mock API) accepts the confirms, not the clocks of the users' devices: the availability check re-runs at confirm and the store accepts one confirm at a time, so even two confirms in the same millisecond produce exactly one winner. Reason: the human's words ("we need to know who submitted first and who second, and tell the second person politely"), (the human's original message is kept in the private work repo). The server-order reading and the idempotency key in REQ-BOOK-009 follow from it and from research.md Q3.
