**Question:** A staff member linked to two dealerships (the demo user "Demo Staff Both Sites", the first staff user the role switch picks) looks at the second dealership's schedule (`/schedule?dealership=dealership-saigon`) and taps a free gap. The gap's link is `/book/for-customer?start&technicianId` (built in the schedule ticket, `gapHref`); it leaves `dealership` out, and so do the "Book for a customer" button and tab. The booking pages then use the person's first dealership (London) with a Saigon technician: the gap note cannot name the technician and the booking goes to the wrong dealership. Should the booking addresses carry the dealership?

- A. Add `dealership` to the gap link and to the "Book for a customer" button and tab when the schedule shows a dealership other than the first. The booking pages already keep `dealership` when it is there (built). It is an id, not personal data (SEC-10 allows ids).
- B. Leave the links as they are: a staff member with two dealerships switches the header's dealership picker on the booking page first.
- C. Take the dealership from the technician (one more look-up on the booking pages).

**Decided:** each staff member works at one dealership only: they see the schedule and book only at their own dealership. A staff member is no longer linked to two dealerships, so the problem in the question goes away. The demo user "Demo Staff Both Sites" is removed from the seed (or tied to one dealership), the dealership picker shows no choice for staff, and the API still refuses a staff member at any other dealership (403). Managers are not changed by this answer. context/security.md "Test users" drops "one staff member tied to both dealerships"; the cross-dealership tests use a staff member of dealership 1 trying dealership 2.
