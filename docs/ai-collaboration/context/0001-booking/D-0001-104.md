**Question:** Small choices in the courtesy car states where the design and the ticket leave a gap. Each is easy to change. Are they OK?

1. **Which search the strip uses.** With the courtesy car chosen, the strip and the chips ask `GET /availability` with `transport=courtesy_car`. With nothing chosen, or with Wait or Drop-off, they ask with `wait`: waiting and drop-off need the same technician and bay, so one search serves both, and switching between them does not reload the strip. (Alternative: send `drop_off` when Drop-off is chosen, which reloads the strip with the same answer.)
2. **"A courtesy car is free if you start at …".** The start is the first time the availability check suggests with a courtesy car (nearest first, D-0001-16). When it is on another day the line says "at 09:00 on Sat 31 Oct"; when the check suggests nothing in the next 14 days the sentence is left out.
3. **While the check is on its way, or if it fails.** The courtesy option stays available. If a courtesy car turns out not to be free, the choice is cleared with the `courtesy-cleared` note; if the check failed, Confirm still checks, and a 409 shows `conflict-courtesy`.
4. **Which 409 is `conflict-courtesy`.** Only a 409 whose reasons are `COURTESY_CAR_BUSY` alone. If the time itself was also taken (a technician or bay reason too), the page shows the usual "was just taken" apology, whose closest times are already times with a courtesy car.
5. **After `conflict-courtesy`.** The time stays chosen (as the prototype draws it) and is not struck through, Confirm stays on, and the car choice is empty. Picking one of the "Closest times with a courtesy car" does not choose the courtesy car again by itself; the customer chooses it, as the alert asks. With no suggestion at all the alert says "There are no other times with a courtesy car in the next 14 days."
6. **`courtesy-cleared` and `no-courtesy-car` together.** When the choice was just cleared, only the cleared note shows (as in the prototype); the "No courtesy car is free" line shows when the courtesy car was not chosen. The cleared note goes once another choice is made. In `conflict-courtesy` the line is not shown either, since the alert says it.
7. **Announcing the line.** The line and the note under the car choice sit in a polite live region, so a screen reader hears why the option went grey without focus moving (A11Y-10).
8. **The gap check on the staff page** (does the job fit the tapped gap) always asks with `wait`, so choosing the courtesy car never makes the page say the job "does not fit" the technician's gap.

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: keep all eight. They follow the ticket's words and the approved pages, and each only fills a gap they leave.
