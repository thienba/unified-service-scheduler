**Question:** D-0001-21 A says the domain works out each technician's free windows and "getSchedule" returns them next to the appointments. The contract has no such field: `GET /appointments` returns a plain list of appointments, and `GET /dealerships/{id}/technicians` returns the weekly pattern and exceptions as local times, not the day's shift and break as instants. What does the schedule screen call to get the free gaps, and each technician's shift, break and leave for the day?

- A. One new read, `GET /dealerships/{dealershipId}/schedule?date=` (operation `getScheduleDay`), for staff and managers of the dealership. It returns the date, the zone, the opening interval (or null when closed), the next open date, the active bays, the day's appointments, and one entry per active technician with `onLeave`, `shift` and `break` as instants and `free` (the windows of 30 minutes or more, worked out in `src/domain/schedule` from the shift, break, opening hours, the rest between jobs, the lead time, the horizon and the grid). The screen makes one call and only draws. Additive: nothing else in the contract changes.
- B. Add `free` to each technician of `GET /dealerships/{id}/technicians`. The screen then makes four calls (dealership, technicians, bays, appointments) and turns the weekly pattern into the day's shift itself, which needs local-time-to-instant work (clock changes) in the screen.
- C. The screen works out the gaps itself (option B of the old Q-0001-32, which D-0001-21 turned down).

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. It is what D-0001-21 describes, keeps the time rules in the domain (AD-9, AD-10), and one call gives one loading and one error state. The C3 build follows A until this is answered.
