**Question:** Is a courtesy car a limited resource that the availability check counts?

- A. Yes: each branch has a fleet of courtesy cars; choosing one requires a car free for the whole job (plus the same break rule does not apply to cars); with none free, the customer can still book as wait or drop-off.
- B. No: the choice is only recorded and shown, never checked.
- C. Also model pick-up and delivery (transport) as a resource.

**Taken:** A, because it reuses the engine's "free for the whole duration" rule with no new concept and makes the choice meaningful; C stays out of scope (provisional, until the brainstorm).
