**Question:** How should the time fields (Start time, Opens, Closes, Starts, Ends, break times) work? Every time is 24-hour and on a 15-minute step.

- A. A list of times. One tap opens a list of `00:00` to `23:45`, 15 minutes apart, and one tap chooses. It opens with the chosen time in view. Every value is valid, so a wrong minute such as 08:07 cannot happen. On a phone it is a bottom sheet.
- B. A field with segments. The hour and the minute are typed or stepped, like the date field. Fast with a keyboard, but any minute can be typed, so the 15-minute rule needs its own error message, and the small hour and minute targets are harder on a phone.
- C. Both: a list that also lets the user type. The most flexible and the most to build and test.

**Decided:** A, the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. The 15-minute step is a rule of the product, and a list makes it impossible to break; it is also the easiest on a phone and matches the drop-downs in the same forms.
