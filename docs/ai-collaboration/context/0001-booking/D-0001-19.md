**Question:** Can garage staff (not the manager) cancel or reschedule a customer's appointment? It does not say it for cancelling, and Q-0001-7 (taken option A) counts cancel among the manager's changes. A customer who phones the garage to cancel would speak to staff.

**Decided:** the recommended option. The recommendation was: A. It follows REQ-BOOK-005.AC-3 and Q-0001-7 as written and keeps three clear roles. If the garage wants staff to handle phone cancels, B is a one-line permission change in the command.
